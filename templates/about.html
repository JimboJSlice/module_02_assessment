About Roamly

Plan. Explore. Remember.

Roamly is a simple travel planning and recording website designed to keep your trips organised in one place.

Your trips, all in one place

Whether you're planning your next adventure or looking back on somewhere you've already visited, Roamly helps you keep track of the details that matter.

Built for travellers

Create trips, organise activities, manage your budget, and keep memories connected to the places you've explored.

What can you do with Roamly?

Everything you need to organise your next adventure.

Plan your trips

Create trips with destinations, dates, descriptions, and budgets.

Organise your itinerary

Add activities and keep track of what you want to do during your trip.

Track your spending

Record expenses and see how much of your travel budget remains.

Remember your adventures

Keep memories connected to individual trips so you can look back on your experiences.
